# Aurel — Héritage

## What it is
A scroll-driven 3D product story for a fictional luxury watch brand, **Aurel**, model **Héritage**.
College / practice project: a portfolio-grade showcase of what a high-end watch launch page can feel like.

## Who it's for
- Reviewers (lecturers, recruiters, peers) judging craft and realism.
- Anyone who opens the link: it must read instantly as a real luxury watch, not a 3D demo.

## The one job
Make a real-looking watch come apart and go back together as you scroll, in the spirit of luxury exploded-view
product films (black studio with a warm amber glow, polished steel, cream dial, parts floating along one axis).

## Truth and constraints
- Brand and specs are fictional. Mark it as a concept piece; don't imitate a real brand's name or logo (no Hublot marks).
- Realism comes from a real rendered product film (supplied by the author), scrubbed frame by frame on scroll. No code-built 3D.
- Static site (HTML/CSS/JS, canvas image sequence), no build step. Must run on a laptop GPU and degrade on mobile.

## Voice
Quiet, precise, confident. Few words. Watchmaking vocabulary used correctly.
