Aurel Héritage concept study

Héritage.

A steel dress watch, taken apart in warm light.

Scroll slowly

Crystal first.
Then the dial.

Each piece lifts away in the order it was set, from sapphire to case.

Every part.
One axis.

Bezel, dial, hands, bridges, wheels and rotor, stacked above the case they live in.

Back together,
by hand.

The same parts return in reverse. The crown sets it running.
